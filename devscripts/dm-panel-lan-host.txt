# DM panel permission check over LAN, host side (an op).
# Run: timeout 300 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/dm-panel-lan-host.txt
# then, once "Started serving on" is in the log, dm-panel-lan-guest.txt as PanelGuest in run-2 (port 25652).
# Expect in this latest.log: "[DmPanel] refused open from PanelGuest" (K as a non-DM), "[DmPanel] refused narrate
# Forged ..." (a forged action packet), then after /dm grant "[DmPanel] PanelGuest opened the panel",
# "[DmPanel] PanelGuest runs /dm narrate Hello from the panel" and "[DM] narrate to ...", and after /dm revoke another
# "[DmPanel] refused narrate ...".
wait 40
closescreen
respawn
/gamemode creative
/publish false survival 25652
/fill 40 149 40 48 149 48 minecraft:stone
/fill 40 150 40 48 153 48 minecraft:air
/tp @s 41.5 150 41.5 0 0
/dm revoke PanelGuest
waitchat guest-ready
/tp @a[name=PanelGuest,limit=1] 44.5 150 42.5 0 10
waitchat forged-sent
wait 20
/dm grant PanelGuest
/say granted
waitchat guest-done
wait 10
/dm revoke PanelGuest
/say revoked
waitchat guest-quit
wait 40
quit
