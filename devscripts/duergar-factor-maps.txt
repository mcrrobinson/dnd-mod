# Checks the Duergar Factor's explorer maps (docs/systems/underdark.md "Explorer maps", design 9 5.3): a Map to a Drow
# Outpost (12 emeralds) and a Map to the Rift (6), both at Unfriendly prices (18 and 9), each leading to the nearest
# such place; and a factor saved before the maps existed gets them the first time someone trades with him.
# It uses a fresh hold of its own (it swaps the hold's factor for a summoned one), far from the other hold scripts'.
# Run: timeout 600 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/duergar-factor-maps.txt
# Expect in latest.log:
# - "[Underdark] map to drow_outpost at X Z" and "[Underdark] map to rift at X Z" when the trades first open
# - the slots readout: "trade 8: 18 emerald -> 1 filled_map {...UnderdarkMap:"drow_outpost"...}" and
#   "trade 9: 9 emerald -> 1 filled_map {...UnderdarkMap:"rift"...}" (base 12 and 6), with Decorations at X Z
# - after buying both, /data get shows both maps in the inventory
# - "/locate structure #dndclasses:underdark_maps/drow_outpost" (and rift) from the same spot: the same X Z as the
#   map lines (locate gives the structure's start chunk corner, the map its centre: within 16 blocks)
# - the summoned "old" factor with one saved trade: the slots readout lists the missing stock and both maps again,
#   each to the next nearest outpost and rift (a map's search skips places an earlier map already led to, like
#   vanilla's explorer maps)
# Screenshots: duergar-factor-maps-trades, -outpost-map, -rift-map, -old-factor
wait 40
closescreen
include _reset.txt
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/gamerule doDaylightCycle false
/difficulty normal
/effect give @s minecraft:night_vision 99999 0 true
/effect give @s minecraft:resistance 99999 4 true
/execute in minecraft:overworld run tp @s 57000 200 -51000
/gamemode spectator
/dndhome duergarhold trade
wait 400
waitserver 2
/rep @s dndclasses:duergar_clans reset
/gamemode survival
/clear @s
/dndhome duergarhold trade
wait 20
use
wait 20
slots
/give @s minecraft:emerald 64
wait 5
trade 8
wait 5
slots
slot 2 quick_move
wait 5
trade 9
wait 5
slot 2 quick_move
wait 5
trade 8
wait 5
scroll -5 -100 0
wait 5
hud off
screenshot duergar-factor-maps-trades
hud on
closescreen
wait 5
hotbar 8
look 0 60
# Long enough for the chat to fade off the map.
wait 220
screenshot duergar-factor-maps-outpost-map
hotbar 7
wait 20
screenshot duergar-factor-maps-rift-map
/data get entity @s Inventory
/locate structure #dndclasses:underdark_maps/drow_outpost
waitserver 2
/locate structure #dndclasses:underdark_maps/rift
waitserver 2
/clear @s

# A factor saved before the maps existed: only the gold trade in his saved offers.
/execute at @e[type=dndclasses:duergar_factor,limit=1,sort=nearest] run summon dndclasses:duergar_factor ~ ~ ~ {NoAI:1b,PersistenceRequired:1b,Tags:["factormaps"],Offers:{Recipes:[{buy:{id:"minecraft:gold_ingot",Count:4b},sell:{id:"minecraft:emerald",Count:1b},maxUses:12,xp:2,priceMultiplier:0.05f}]}}
/kill @e[type=dndclasses:duergar_factor,tag=!factormaps,distance=..12]
wait 10
/dndhome duergarhold trade
wait 20
use
wait 20
slots
hud off
screenshot duergar-factor-maps-old-factor
hud on
closescreen
/kill @e[tag=factormaps]
quit
