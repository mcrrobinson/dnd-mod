# Dragon Lairs
The home of a [Lightning Chaser](../mobs/dragons.md), on the very summit of a mountain.

![A dragon lair on a snowy summit: standing stones with lightning rods round the nest, and its Lightning Chaser flying below](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/mcrrobinson/dnd-mod/pr-screenshots/docs-screenshots/dragon-lair.png)

## How it works
- The lair sits on the highest point of a jagged, frozen or stony peak, with the mountain falling away on every side.
- It's a ring of standing stones crowned with lightning rods, round a nest of logs and bones heaped with gold and a hoard chest.
- The floor is scorched stone, with fulgurite where the lightning has come down and a ring of chiselled deepslate runes. The nest is woven Dragon Nest, its hoard gold and raw gold blocks, Treasury Coin Piles and the Lightning Chaser's shed scales.
- **Hoard chest (`chests/dragon_lair`):** gold, copper, emeralds, diamonds and enchanted diamond gear, sometimes an enchanted golden apple, a trident or a Staff of Lightning. Every hoard chest also holds one magic item, Rare (70%) or Very Rare (30%), and 6% of them a pair of Storm-Chaser Boots (see [Magic loot](../systems/magic-loot.md)).
- **Side cache**: against the rim, north or south of the nest, a cairn of deepslate bricks holds a second hoard chest behind a 2x2x2 **Heavy Boulder** (Hard, DC 15). Only a Barbarian can heave it aside (Athletics, with advantage while raging); anyone else blows it up with one TNT, which raises an alarm ([Class-gated obstacles](../systems/obstacles.md)). Lairs generated before it was added don't have one.
- Each lair starts with one Lightning Chaser (sometimes two). It never strays more than about 24 blocks before circling back.
- Once it's killed, the lair waits **3 in-game days** and then sends a new one (structure spawn override; never in peaceful or with another Lightning Chaser within 64 blocks). A chaser that left without dying (e.g. in peaceful) is replaced as soon as possible.

### Building blocks
The lair is dressed with its own blocks ([Building blocks](../dev/building-blocks.md)), all in the **DnD Building Blocks** creative tab:

| Block | Where | Notes |
|-|-|-|
| Fulgurite | 10% of the scorched floor, and round the foot of each standing stone (3 in 4 of the floor blocks beside it) | dark glassy rock with tan and white fused streaks; 8 sand round a lightning rod make 8 |
| Fulgurite Spike | beside 1 in 5 of the floor blocks round each standing stone, and the odd one (1 in 20) on the floor between the nest and the rim | a cross model with no collision; needs a solid block below; 2 fulgurite stacked make 4 |
| Storm Glass | the top block of every standing stone that still has its lightning rod, under the copper cap | animated: white cracks of lightning flicker across dark blue-grey glass; light level 2; like glass, only silk touch keeps it. 8 glass round a Storm Dragon Scale make 8 |
| Storm Glass Pane | not placed | 6 storm glass make 16 |
| Dragon Nest (slab) | the nest floor; 35% of the blocks in the ring of logs, and some of the slabs on top of it | woven branches with bones and scales; axe; burns. 5 sticks and 4 bones make 2 |
| Treasury Coin Pile | the hoard: heaps of 3-7 layers among the gold blocks, and loose 1-3 layer piles round them | the Dwarven Fortresses' block, 1-8 layers; drops gold nuggets (see [Dwarven Fortresses](dwarven-fortresses.md#building-blocks)) |
| Shed Storm Scales | heaps of 1-4 layers in the hoard, and 1-2 layer piles on about a fifth of the floor just outside the nest | yellow and blue scales in layers like snow (shovel); each layer drops one **Storm Dragon Scale**, never the block. 3 scales make 3 layers |

Lairs generated before these blocks were added keep their old floor and nest. Where the new blocks go is picked from a hash of the position, not the lair's random, so a lair that was half generated by an older version still lines up.

## Where to find it
- Jagged Peaks, Frozen Peaks, Stony Peaks. Structure set spacing 12 chunks, separation 4, and at least 6 chunks from dwarven fortresses (which share the peaks).
- Frozen Peaks also have [frost lairs](frost-lairs.md), the Frost Drake's version in snow and ice, which keep at least 6 chunks from dragon lairs.

## Commands
- `/locate structure dndclasses:dragon_lair`

## For developers
- Blocks: the families `FULGURITE` and `DRAGON_NEST` in `ModBlockFamilies`, the rest in `LairBlocks` (`FulguriteSpikeBlock`, `ScalePileBlock`); textures and their resources from `tools/lair_block_textures.py`. Devscripts: `lair-blocks.txt` (each block, drops, recipes), `lair-blocks-locate.txt` and `lair-blocks-survey.txt` (a newly generated lair of each kind).
- `world/gen/lair/` (`DragonLairStructure`, `LairPiece`, `LairRespawns` for the per-lair respawn timer). `LairPiece.sideCache` builds the cairn from its own seeded random, so older lairs keep their layout. Frost lairs are the same code with `LairPiece.Kind.FROST`. Devscripts: `dragon-lair-locate.txt`, `dragon-lair-survey.txt`, `lightning-chaser-lair-lookup.txt`.
