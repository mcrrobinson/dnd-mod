# stonecutter, a few recipes, the lockable Chain Cage, the Spidersilk Curtain's slow, and the Duergar Brazier.
# Run: timeout 300 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/underdark-building-blocks.txt

# - each /loot line drops its own block ("Dropped 1 [...]"), the slabs two when double
# - stonecutter on Faerzress Stone: button 0 -> Polished Faerzress, button 1 -> 2 Polished Faerzress Slab (slot 1)

#   rigged 2 fails ("the lock holds"), a rigged 18 picks it ("Test passed" on open=true,locked=false); in creative
#   use toggles even a locked cage
# - curtain: `speed` on open floor about 0.21-0.22 blocks/tick, inside the curtains about 0.065 (30%)
# - brazier: the zombie standing in it loses health (Health below 20 after 5 s); a Duergar Soldier keeps its full (30)
