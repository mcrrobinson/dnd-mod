# Deck of Many Things (docs/items/magic-items.md#deck-of-many-things), rigged with /dndmagic deck @s next <card>. Run:
#   timeout 400 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/deck.txt
# Expect in latest.log:
#  1. declare: actionbar "You will draw 3 cards."
#  2. Sun: "[Deck] Dev draws The Sun (1/3)", /dndclass progress shows level 1 (40 XP), "The deck gives you" a Rare item
#  3. Moon: "grants you N wish(es)"; a forced 1 on /dm save becomes "[D20] Dev spends a Moon wish: rerolling 1" and a
#     natural 20 (CRITICAL)
#  4. Euryale (3/3): the sheet shows "Euryale (Deck of Many Things)" -2 on saves, "[Deck] Dev's deck vanishes", no deck
#     left; reading a Scroll of Remove Curse: "Euryale's curse lifts." and the sheet line is gone
#  5. Skull: an Avatar rises; a wolf's blow is ignored ("ignores Wolf's blow", Health 60.0) and calls a second Avatar
#     (count 2); the player's blow lands (Health 50.0)
#  6. Talons (2/2): the Ring of Protection (Rare) crumbles, the Eye of the Tyrant (Legendary) stays
#  7. Fool: a level's XP lost and "(1/1)" then a second draw "(2/2)"; Gem drops 20 diamonds and 10 emeralds at your feet
#     (picked up: /clear ... 0 finds 20 and 10)
