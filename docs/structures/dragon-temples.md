# Dragon Temples
The Shrine of the Three Breaths: a terrace set into a mountain slope near a dragon lair, reached by a grand stair, with a courtyard of three great dragon statues, a double-roofed sanctum of breath altars, a monks' hall and a pagoda library tower, and a training yard. [Dragonborn](../mobs/dragonborn.md) live here. Temples are the Dragonborn home (see [Racial homes](../races/racial-homes.md)).

## How it works
Every temple is the same plan, turned to climb into its slope:

| Piece | What it is |
|-|-|
| Slope | Built first: reshapes the ground round the terrace so it sits in the mountain instead of cutting into it. Over the terrace the slope is cleared. Along the back and sides, where the ground is higher than the floor, a retaining wall of rough natural stone (cobblestone, stone bricks, andesite, a little moss) rises 3 blocks at the terrace edge, with a ledge behind it out to 3.5 blocks. Beyond that the ground is cut back to a slope climbing about a block per block (steepening by 0.06 x distance squared, with a little noise), out to 20 blocks. Each cut column keeps its own surface (snow, grass, stone, packed ice) and the block under it, and the cut fades out (at most 1.5 blocks per block) towards the edge, so no sheer face is left. Where the ground drops away below the sides and front, it's built up into an embankment of the column's own ground, sloping down a block per block from 3 below the floor (5 at the front). Natural generation then lays snow on the new ground. |
| Terrace | 39 wide and 30 deep, 8-12 blocks above the stair foot. Polished deepslate floor, built up beneath with cobbled deepslate faced with deepslate bricks, a polished blackstone parapet with gilded posts along the open edges, Ember incense braziers at the front corners. |
| Grand stair | 5 wide, one step up per block from the foot to the courtyard: deepslate tile steps, polished blackstone edges, balustrades with a lantern every fourth step on an Ancestor Relief facing the stair (gilded at the foot), Ember incense braziers either side of the foot |
| Courtyard | 21 by 15 at the top of the stair. A gilded blackstone path runs from the stair, parts round the great Ember statue and meets again at the sanctum's steps. Frost and Storm incense-brazier posts at the back corners, paper lanterns on lacquered fence posts by the path at the stair and by the sanctum steps. |
| Statues | Three dragons sitting up on their haunches, carved from their ancestry's Dragonstone with Dragon Belly plates down the chest and neck, glowing Dragonstone Eyes, ivory Dragon Horns (horns, claws, fangs, spines, tail tip) and wing membranes of their Dragonscale Tiles. Each has its forelegs planted, its tail curled round behind, its neck curving up and forward so its open-jawed head looks out past the plinth, and its wings raised and spread. **Ember**, the great one in the middle of the path: 18 blocks tall on a two-tier 7 by 9 plinth (Ember tiles with gilded corners, then polished blackstone), wings 13 blocks across over the paths. **Frost** and **Storm**, the guardians at the front corners: 15 blocks tall on 5 by 7 plinths of their tiles, wings 9 across. |
| Sanctum (hearth) | 21 by 15 at the back, against the mountain. A hall on a one-block podium with broad steps across its front, under a double-eaved hip roof of Ember Dragonscale Tiles striped Frost and Storm over their altars, both roofs flaring out at the eaves and sweeping up at the corners. A colonnade of Temple Lacquer pillars on chiseled blackstone bases with gold capitals, lacquered brackets and an architrave, runs round the outside under the lower roof. The hall's walls stand 2 blocks in: lacquered posts framing deepslate panels, lattice windows of lacquer fence, a lacquer band and a chiseled deepslate frieze, and an arched door under a gold lintel with an Ancestor Relief above. Over them a clerestory of lattice windows carries the upper roof, whose ridge has a blackstone crest between gilded finials with Dragon Horns and an Ember incense brazier smoking in the middle. Inside, the hall is open up to the clerestory, crossed by lacquered beams hung with soul lanterns, with Ancestor Reliefs on the side walls; the three breath altars stand along the back wall under red, light blue and blue banners bearing the gold [Three Breaths](../items/banner-patterns.md), the reliquary behind the Ember altar. 21 blocks tall. |
| Cloister | Down one side, two buildings of whitewashed calcite between lacquered timbers on a deepslate footing, under Storm Dragonscale roofs. **The monks' hall** at the front: long and low under a gabled roof whose ridge runs back from the valley, with a blackstone crest and Dragon Horns at both ends, a lattice window in its front gable under an Ancestor Relief. Inside, a cross-hall with the door onto the courtyard and a corridor back between six small rooms (4-6 monks' cells with a Temple Lacquer floor, a red bed, a chest and a candle; the rest storerooms with barrels), 12 prayer wheels set into the corridor walls, paper lanterns overhead, lattice windows in the outer walls. **A covered walk** of lacquer leads back to **the library tower**: a three-storey pagoda, each storey smaller than the one below under its own skirt of eaves with paper lanterns at the lowest corners, topped with a pyramid roof and a gold and horn finial (22 blocks tall). Its ground floor holds the temple's enchanting table in a ring of 22 bookshelves (enough for level 30 enchantments) and 10 more along the back wall; a ladder climbs to the reading room above, with two lecterns back to back, shelves and a candle. |
| Training yard | Down the other side: coarse dirt and gravel, seven sparring posts (four along the parapet between paper lanterns on lacquered posts, three in the yard), three armour stands in the Knight set (one complete, two partial), a grindstone, an anvil and a barrel |

### Building blocks
The temple is built from its own blocks, all in the **DnD Building Blocks** creative tab. Each drops itself when mined.

| Block | Shapes | What it is | Recipe |
|-|-|-|-|
| Ember, Frost and Storm Dragonscale Tiles | block, stairs, slab | overlapping rounded scales: deep red to orange, pale blue to white, teal to dark blue with yellow glints. Stone (pickaxe), like deepslate tiles. | 8 deepslate tiles round a red, light blue or cyan dye make 8 |
| Temple Lacquer Planks | planks, stairs, slab, fence | glossy red-lacquered boards with black joints. Wood (axe), like dark oak. | 8 dark oak planks round a red dye make 8 |
| Ember-, Frost- and Storm-Scorched Stone | block | basalt with red glaze and glowing cracks, frost rime and pale blue cracks, or branching yellow-white scorch lines. Like smooth basalt. | 8 smooth basalt round a blaze powder, snowball or copper ingot make 8 |
| Ancestor Relief | block that faces you | carved grey-black stone with a dragon's head in profile and a gold eye. Like chiseled deepslate. | 4 chiseled deepslate round a gold nugget make 4 |
| Prayer Wheel | | a red drum with gold script bands between brass caps. Right-click it and it turns for 3 seconds (a creak and a chime; right-clicking again keeps it going). Pickaxe. | copper ingot, red wool, copper ingot in a column |
| Ember, Frost and Storm Incense Brazier | | a dark bronze bowl on legs with embers glowing in its colour, giving off smoke of that colour. Light 13. Doesn't burn or cook. Pickaxe. | 4 copper ingots, a coal and a red, light blue or cyan dye (`ICI` / `IDI`) |
| Paper Lantern | | a cream paper lantern with red caps. Stands on a block or hangs under one (place it against a ceiling), like a lantern. Light 14. | string over paper, torch, paper over paper |

| Ember, Frost and Storm Dragon Belly | block, stairs, slab | the statues' chests and necks: broad curved plates, gold, white or sea-green. Like polished deepslate. | a Dragonstone and a bone meal |
| Ember, Frost and Storm Dragonstone Eye | block that faces you | Dragonstone with a glowing slit-pupilled eye (orange, ice blue or yellow) under a heavy brow on its front. Light 9. Like polished deepslate. | glowstone dust over a Dragonstone |
| Dragon Horn | points away from the face it's placed on, like an end rod | a curved ivory horn with growth ridges, darker at the base: the statues' horns, claws, fangs and spines. Like a bone block. | 3 bones in a diagonal make 2 |
| Sparring Post | block that faces you | a straw-and-rope wrapped post with a padded front. Hitting it doesn't break it: the action bar shows what the swing would deal, e.g. "Sparring Post: 7.0 damage (100% swing)". Sneak to break it. Axe. | white wool, hay bale, oak fence in a column |

The Sparring Post works the damage out as vanilla does against an unarmoured mob: your attack damage (weapon, Strength, class and gear modifiers), the weapon's damage enchantments (Sharpness, not Smite or Bane), how charged the swing is (counted from your last hit on a post) and a jump crit (x1.5). It doesn't count effects that only trigger on hitting a creature (Sneak Attack, smites and other on-hit extras). Holding the button down counts a hit every 5 ticks at most.

The Elder sells 16 Ember, Frost or Storm Dragonscale Tiles for 3 emeralds each (2 for Dragonborn).

### People
5 to 7 Dragonborn Templars when the temple generates (two in the courtyard and sometimes a third at the top of the stair, one on the sanctum's colonnade by the door, one in the monks' hall, one or two in the yard) and the Dragonborn Elder in the sanctum. Templars spawn naturally on the terrace later, up to 7 within 48 blocks. No monsters spawn inside a temple's box. See [Dragonborn](../mobs/dragonborn.md).

### Temple law
Neutral to everyone. Every Dragonborn within 16 blocks (24 if you're Unfriendly or worse with the Dragonsworn) who can see you takes offence, and the Templars turn on you for 30-50 s, if you:

- break a block of a statue, or an altar's plinth, fire or rod (a Dragonscale Tiles block in the sanctum counts as a plinth)
- open the reliquary (any chest in the sanctum)

Each seen offence costs 40 standing with the Dragonsworn. There's no kin trust: Dragonborn are held to the same law. Striking any Dragonborn brings every Templar within 16 blocks down on you. Helping to drive off a goblin raid on the temple forgives you.

### Shrines (Dragonborn only)
- **Blessing of the Ancestors**: right-click the altar of your own ancestry. Once per in-game day you're blessed for 20 minutes: your [Breath Weapon](../races/breath-weapon.md)'s cooldown is halved (60 s becomes 30 s) and it deals +2 damage. Using it again the same day says "Your ancestors have already blessed you today."
- **Rite of Rebirth**: right-click another ancestry's altar while holding 10 diamonds in your main hand. The diamonds are used up and your ancestry becomes the altar's (Ember, Frost or Storm), with its breath and snout colour. Any blessing ends. Without the diamonds the altar tells you the price. This and `/dndrace set` are the only ways to change ancestry.
- Other races get "The altar stays cold to you." and the altar does nothing.

## For your race
Temples are the Dragonborn home. A Dragonborn gets:

- a "Welcome home, Dragonborn" title on arriving
- Regeneration I in the sanctum while no hostile mob is within 16 blocks
- 25% off the Elder's trades
- Kin stock: the Elder sells Dragonborn the [Draconic Scale](../items/armor.md#racial-armor) set (helmet 12, chestplate 20, leggings 18, boots 10 before kin prices); nobody else sees those trades
- the Blessing of the Ancestors and the Rite of Rebirth
- a Friendly (150) start with the Dragonsworn

## Where to find it
Jagged Peaks, Stony Peaks, Frozen Peaks, Snowy Slopes and Groves. Temples use a spacing of 20 chunks and a separation of 7, and only generate:

- where a dragon lair or frost lair really generates (its own peak check passes, and its grid slot isn't in the lair set's exclusion zone, e.g. dragon lairs within 6 chunks of a dwarven fortress grid slot) within 10 chunks of the temple's start chunk, so every temple has a lair nearby
- at least 4 chunks from any dwarven fortress that really generates
- on a slope: a stair foot with open ground below it, and the ground under the terrace 6-18 blocks higher on average, within 12 blocks above or 14 below the terrace floor everywhere

Goblins sometimes [raid](../systems/goblin-raids.md) temples. The war party climbs up from the valley to the courtyard and the Templars rush out to meet it.

## Commands
- `/locate structure dndclasses:dragon_temple`
- `/dndhome temple [ember|frost|storm]` (operators): teleports you into the nearest temple's sanctum, two blocks in front of that altar and looking at it (a temple you placed with `templeplace` within 160 blocks first)
- `/dndhome templeplace` (operators): builds a Dragon Temple on the nearest chunk within 6 chunks whose terrain and biome give a site, skipping the lair and fortress rules, and records it like a generated one, for trying temples on different ground. It builds into chunks that already exist, so snow isn't laid on the reshaped slope as it is in natural generation.
- `/dndhome templesurvey [grid] [spacing]` (operators): finds the nearest temple from each point of a grid round you (default 3 x 3, 2000 blocks apart) and checks a dragon or frost lair starts within 10 chunks of it. Each temple logs `[TempleSurvey] temple at X Z: dragon lair N chunks, frost lair M chunks: PASS` (or FAIL), then a summary.
- `/dndhome pieces` lists every piece of the temple you're in.

## Known limitations
- Killing lair dragons doesn't raise standing with the Dragonsworn yet: the dragons aren't a faction.
- The Elder's maps look for a lair within 100 chunks the first time someone opens his trades, which can stall the server for a moment. With no lair in range that map trade is left out.
- Steep mountainsides don't qualify as sites (the climb under the terrace must be at most 18 blocks), so temples sit on the gentler slopes.
- The slope blending works column by column: a tree standing just outside a cut column can be left with part of its crown over the cut. Very steep peaks still rise steeply beyond the 20-block margin.
- Temples generated before this rework keep their old buildings; chunks of an old temple that hadn't generated yet build with the new pieces.
- The enchanting table moved from the sanctum to the library tower.
