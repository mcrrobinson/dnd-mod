# Rival losses under one point carry over (docs/systems/factions.md "Rivals"): four +1 gold trades with the Duergar
# Factor give the Grey Clans +4 and cost the Mountain Dwarves 2 (-0.5 each), the drow and the Tinkers' Guild 1 each
# (-0.25 each). Before the fix, Math.round(-0.5) and Math.round(-0.25) were 0, so they lost nothing.
# Run: timeout 600 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/rival-rounding.txt
# Each step takes one result with a plain click (a shift-click would make every trade the gold pays for).
# Expect in latest.log (the day skip lets grudges decay 5 first: Grey Clans -245, drow -695 at the first trade):
# - action bar per trade: "+1 The Grey Clans" on trades 1 and 3, "+1 The Grey Clans, -1 Mountain Dwarves" on trade 2,
#   "-1 Mountain Dwarves, and 3 others" on trade 4 (the drow and the Tinkers' Guild lose their first point)
# - "/rep @s" after each trade: Grey Clans -244/-243/-242/-241, Mountain Dwarves 0/-1/-1/-2, drow -695 until -696
#   and Tinkers 150 until 149 on trade 4
# - the DndRepRivalCarry compound in the player data is empty again after four trades (every carry came to a whole point)
wait 40
closescreen
include _reset.txt
/gamerule doMobSpawning false
/gamerule doDaylightCycle false
/effect give @s minecraft:night_vision 99999 0 true
/effect give @s minecraft:resistance 99999 4 true
/execute in minecraft:overworld run tp @s 57000 200 -51000
/gamemode spectator
/dndhome duergarhold trade
wait 400
waitserver 2
# A new day, so the Grey Clans' daily trade cap (15) from earlier runs doesn't stop the gains.
/time add 24000
/rep @s dndclasses:duergar_clans reset
/rep @s dndclasses:mountain_dwarves reset
/rep @s dndclasses:drow reset
/rep @s dndclasses:tinkers_guild reset
/gamemode survival
/clear @s
/dndhome duergarhold trade
wait 20
# The gold trade has 12 uses; restock it so the script can run again on the same factor.
/data modify entity @e[type=dndclasses:duergar_factor,limit=1,sort=nearest] Offers.Recipes[2].uses set value 0
/rep @s
wait 5
use
wait 20
slots
/give @s minecraft:gold_ingot 64
wait 5
trade 2
wait 5
slot 2 pickup
wait 10
/rep @s
wait 5
trade 2
wait 5
slot 2 pickup
wait 10
/rep @s
wait 5
trade 2
wait 5
slot 2 pickup
wait 10
/rep @s
wait 5
trade 2
wait 5
slot 2 pickup
wait 10
/rep @s
wait 5
closescreen
/rep @s
waitserver 2
/data get entity @s
waitserver 2
quit
