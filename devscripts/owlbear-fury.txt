# Grimclaw's fury: a furious owlbear (tag dndclasses.owlbear_fury, as DungeonChampion gives the Goblin Warren's
# Grimclaw) charges every 80 ticks below half health instead of 120. A plain owlbear stays at 120.
# Run: timeout 400 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/owlbear-fury.txt
# The owlbear is put back 10 blocks from the player every 10 ticks, so every charge misses and it keeps charging.
# Check run/logs/debug.log for "[MobAction] Grimclaw charge (cooldown N)" lines and their t= world times:
#  phase 1 (150/150 HP): cooldown 120, about 170 ticks apart (20 wind-up + 30 charge + 120)
#  phase 2 (60/150 HP):  cooldown 80, about 130 ticks apart
#  phase 3 (a plain owlbear, "Plain Owlbear", 20/50 HP): cooldown 120 again

respawn
