# Desolate fortress gates: the outside battered by the siege, at every level, in fresh land at x 200000+.
# Run: timeout 1500 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/desolate-fortress-gate.txt (move Z for a world where these have generated).
# Checks: screenshots fortress-gate-<level> (in front), -close and -side; "[Home] dndclasses:dwarven_gate ... facing" in the log;
# "[Desolation] dndclasses:desolate_dwarven_fortress (<LEVEL>)" lines in <run dir>/logs/debug.log.
