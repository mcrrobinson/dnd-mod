# Dragonborn Breath Weapon
Dragonborn breathe their ancestry's element in a short cone on the **racial key (R)**: fire (Ember), frost (Frost) or lightning (Storm). It recharges in 60 seconds, or straight away on a rest.

## How it works
- **Key**: R, listed as "Racial Power (Breath Weapon)" under D&D Classes in Controls. The key is generic: it fires your race's active power. Only Dragonborn have one so far; anyone else gets "Your race has no racial power".
- **Cone**: 6 blocks from your eyes, 60° wide (30° either side of where you look). You need a clear line from your eyes to each target's eyes; walls and blocks stop it.
- **Damage**: 6 + ⌊class level / 2⌋, so 6 at level 0-1 up to 11 at level 10. Classless players breathe for 6.
- **Who it hits**: every living thing in the cone except you, your party members and your own pets. Other players are hit only if PvP is on.
- **Saves**: each target makes a DEX save against **DC 8 + your CON modifier + your proficiency bonus** (shown on the save lane as "Dragonborn breath"). A success halves the damage and weakens the rider (see below). Creatures immune to the element (fire-immune mobs against Ember, for example) take nothing and don't roll.

| Ancestry | Damage type | Rider | On a successful save |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ember** | fire (`dndclasses:dragonborn_fire`, tagged `is_fire`) | Sets targets alight for 5 s. Each ray of the breath that hits a flammable block has a 15% chance to light it (only with `doFireTick` and `mobGriefing` on). | Burns for 2.5 s |
| **Frost** | freezing (`dndclasses:frost_breath`, tagged `is_freezing`) | Freeze for 3 s. Water sources in the cone with air above turn into frosted ice, so you can freeze a bridge. | No Freeze |
| **Storm** | lightning (`dndclasses:dragonborn_lightning`, tagged `is_lightning`) | Glowing for 2 s. The bolt arcs from the nearest target to one more creature within 4 blocks of it, for half damage (that creature saves too). | Half damage |

- **Cooldown**: 60 s. Pressing R early shows "Breath Weapon recharges in N s" on the action bar. A short or long rest recharges it at once. Creative players ignore the cooldown.
- **Survives death and rejoining**: the cooldown is stored with your character, so dying or logging out doesn't reset it.
- **HUD**: a small icon (flame, snowflake or bolt) sits just right of the mana bar. While recharging it greys out from the top and clears as the cooldown runs down. It's only shown to Dragonborn, and only while `dndRaces` is on.
- **Visuals**: everyone nearby sees the cone: dragon flame puffs (Ember), frost puffs and snowflakes (Frost, the Frost Drake's particles) or a crackle of electric sparks (Storm), plus sparks along the arc.

## Where to find it
Pick Dragonborn and an ancestry in the race picker, or have an operator run `/dndrace set <player> dragonborn ember|frost|storm`.

## Commands
- `/dndrace set <player> dragonborn <ancestry>` changes ancestry (and so the breath element).
- `/dndclass rest <player> short` recharges the breath (as any rest does).

## Configuration
- Gamerule `dndRaces`: off means no race powers, so the key does nothing and the icon is hidden.
- Ember's block fires follow `doFireTick` and `mobGriefing`.

## Known limitations
- The Dragon Temple's Blessing of the Ancestors (half cooldown, +2 damage) and the Rite of Rebirth come with the Dragon Temple.
- Ember doesn't burn cobwebs or vines yet (an obstacle hook for later).
- Draconic Resistance (half damage of your element) is part of the racial traits ticket, not this one.

## For developers
- Server: `classes/Race/BreathWeapon`. `register()` is called from `RaceLifecycle.register()`; `RaceLifecycle` calls `BreathWeapon.sync` on join, respawn, race change and `dndRaces` changes.
- Packets: `dndclasses:racial_power` (C2S, empty), `dndclasses:racial_breath` (S2C to the breather and everyone tracking them: entity id, ancestry, eye position, look vector) and `dndclasses:racial_breath_cooldown` (S2C to the breather: usable, ancestry, ticks left, cooldown length).
- Saves go through `DragonSaves.save` (one roll per target per breath, exposure tag `racial_breath`) and `SaveResult.damage`/`duration`.
- The cooldown is an overworld game time in the persistent compound: `dndRace.breathReadyAt` (long) and `dndRace.breathCooldown` (the last cooldown length, for the HUD). `ClassLifecycle`'s COPY_FROM copies it on death. A rest clears it through `RestEvents.AFTER_REST`.
- Hook for later features: `BreathWeapon.MODIFY` (a Fabric event) can change a breath's `Stats(damage, cooldownTicks)` before it fires. `BreathWeapon.reset(player)` readies it.
- Client: `Client/BreathWeaponClient` (key, particles, HUD icon drawn from `PowerupOverlay`). Icons are generated by `tools/breath_icons.py`.
- Devscript: `devscripts/dragonborn-breath.txt` (all three ancestries on frozen zombies, frosted ice check, cooldown after death).
