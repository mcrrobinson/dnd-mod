# A desolate Cogsdeep's caved-in observatory dome at every desolation level, in fresh land at x 260000-280000.
# Run: timeout 900 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/desolate-observatory.txt
# (move the /tp points to fresh land for a world where these have generated already).
# Checks: screenshots dob-<level>-top (from above the door), -side (level with the rim), -inside (from the floor), -inside-high (over the floor),
# -gallery (on the gallery by the telescope), -telescope (behind it) and -stair (by the stairwell's rail); "Pos" after /dndhome cogsdeep
# observatory and the "[Home]" lines say which burrow it found.
