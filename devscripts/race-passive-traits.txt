# Run: timeout 600 ./gradlew runClient --offline -PdevScript=devscripts/race-passive-traits.txt
# Expect in latest.log ([CHAT] lines):
#  - Fire: Human loses 4 health to /damage 4 in_fire, Tiefling 2; Ember Dragonborn 2 (in_fire), Storm 2
#    (lightning_bolt), Frost 2 (freeze); Gnome 2.8 to magic, Human 4.

#  - /dndrace traits: deepslate break speed 1.00 for a Human, 1.20 for a Dwarf.
#  - Trance: the time_since_rest score after 10 s is under 20 for an Elf, about 200 for a Human.
#  - Second Breakfast: bread raises foodLevel by 5 for a Human, 6 for a Halfling.
#  - Tinker: 64 rail crafts give a Human exactly 1024 rails; a Gnome more (about 1040) with "[Race] ... Tinker" lines.
