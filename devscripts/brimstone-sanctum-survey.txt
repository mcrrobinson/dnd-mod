# Brimstone Sanctum placement survey and shots, in the Nether.
# Run: timeout 900 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/brimstone-sanctum-survey.txt (add -PdevWorld=<world> for
# another seed; the survey area, 12000 12000 in the Nether, must be unexplored so the chunks generate fresh).
# Checks, in <run dir>/logs/latest.log:
# - the [CHAT] line of /locate (nearest sanctum)
# - "[SanctumSurvey] sanctum at X Y Z: biome ..., lava under 0 of N columns, cut ceiling over C: PASS" for every
#   sanctum found from a 3x3 grid of points 1500 blocks apart, and "[SanctumSurvey] n sanctums, n pass, 0 fail"
# - "[Home] Brimstone Sanctum at chunk ..." from /dndhome sanctum; screenshots bs-top (aerial), bs-side (side
#   view of the platform and pillars), bs-gate, bs-hall, bs-refuge, bs-market, bs-den
# - "Test passed" counts of Exiles (4-6 when freshly generated) and the Pactbroker within 48 blocks
wait 40
closescreen
/gamemode spectator
/execute in minecraft:the_nether run tp @s 12000 100 12000
wait 100
/locate structure dndclasses:brimstone_sanctum
waitserver 2
/dndhome sanctumsurvey 3 1500
waitserver 2
/dndhome sanctum top
wait 300
hud off
screenshot bs-top
/dndhome sanctum side
wait 100
screenshot bs-side
/dndhome sanctum gate
wait 80
screenshot bs-gate
/dndhome sanctum hall
wait 80
screenshot bs-hall
/dndhome sanctum anchor
wait 60
screenshot bs-refuge
/dndhome sanctum market
wait 60
screenshot bs-market
/dndhome sanctum den
wait 60
screenshot bs-den
hud on
/execute at @s if entity @e[type=dndclasses:tiefling_exile,distance=..48]
/execute at @s if entity @e[type=dndclasses:tiefling_pactbroker,distance=..48]
quit
