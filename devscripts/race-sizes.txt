# Racial body sizes: hitbox, eye height, gaps, the dndRaces gamerule, Wild Shape and head features.
# Run: timeout 600 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/race-sizes.txt
# Expect in latest.log ([DevScript] size lines, client and server agree):
#  - human 0.600x1.800 eye 1.620; elf/halforc/tiefling the same; dwarf 0.600x1.530 eye 1.377;
#    halfling/gnome 0.390x1.170 eye 1.053; dragonborn 0.600x1.890 eye 1.701. Marker Pos[1] = 150 + eye.
#  - Halfling sneak-walks through the 1x1 tunnel (z ends past 207) and stays CROUCHING (0.390x0.975) when it
#    lets go of sneak inside; a Human is stopped at the tunnel mouth (z about 202.7).
#  - Dwarf in the 1.5-high gap is CROUCHING (eye 1.080), STANDING once out; Dragonborn STANDING in a 2-high tunnel.
#  - /gamerule dndRaces false: the halfling is 0.600x1.800 again; true: 0.390x1.170.
#  - Wolf Wild Shape as a halfling: the wolf's 0.600x0.850, back to 0.390x1.170 when it ends.
# Screenshots: size-<race> (F5 front, hitboxes, a white+red wool pole two blocks high), crawl-halfling,
#  crawl-human, dwarf-gap, face-<race> close-ups (FOV 30), wild-shape-halfling-wolf and -after.
