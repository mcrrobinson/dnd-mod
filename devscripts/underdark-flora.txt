# Checks the Underdark's flora (design 9 T3; docs/items/underdark-blocks.md, docs/systems/underdark.md): the new
# blocks and their drops, harvesting Ripplebark, a Barrelstalk's water, spores and a Bluecap growing giant fungi with
# bone meal, Roasted Trillimac (smelting and eating), Glow Spores brewing Night Vision, the explorer maps, and in the
# Underdark the giant fungi, the Darklake's lake life and mist, and the Underdark music event.
# Run: timeout 600 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/underdark-flora.txt
# The map check needs a test data pack in the run dir's world (not committed): run/saves/New World/datapacks/
# flora-maptest with pack.mcmeta (pack_format 12) and data/dndclasses/tags/worldgen/structure/underdark_maps/
# drow_outpost.json = {"replace":false,"values":["#minecraft:village"]}, so the drow outpost map leads to a village.
# Expect in latest.log:
# - screenshots underdark-flora-showcase, underdark-flora-grown, underdark-flora-forest-1/-2, underdark-flora-darklake
# - /loot: grown Ripplebark 2-3, young 1; Glowing Spore Drip -> Glow Spores; Pearl-Clam -> Darklake Pearl (silk touch:
#   the clam); Barrelstalk lower half 1, upper half none; Trillimac -> 1-2 Trillimac Slices; Faerie Lichen (shears) 1
# - "Test passed" for: harvested Ripplebark back at age 0; the used Barrelstalk filled=false; stems over the
#   zurkhwood spores, the nightlight spores and the Bluecap; a filled Barrelstalk giving a bucket
# - `slots` after the Barrelstalk: a water bottle (minecraft:potion) and the second glass bottle unused; then a
#   water bucket
# - "Successfully filled" counts for the grown fungi's caps (zurkhwood caps, nightlight caps)
# - the furnace's Items: dndclasses:roasted_trillimac; the brewing stand's: minecraft:night_vision
# - foodLevel goes up by 6 after eating Roasted Trillimac
# - [EventMusic] ... -> UNDERDARK on arriving in the Underdark
# - [Underdark] flora ... counts with giant fungi in the Fungal Forest and lake life in the Darklake
# - [Underdark] Darklake mist on, at the lake shore
# - the maps: "No rift to map" and "No duergar_hold to map"; with the test pack, a map named "Map to a Drow Outpost"
#   from the command and from the loot table
