# Shared adventuring blocks (docs/items/adventuring-blocks.md): tavern tables and benches placed by hand join up,
# a named signpost shows its name, the Wanted Poster reads out and drops off its wall, and a Bedroll gives a long
# rest without setting the spawn point.
# Run: timeout 400 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/shared-blocks.txt
# Check logs/latest.log:
# - "Test passed" for every /execute if block line (6 joins, the poster falling off, the dropped bedroll head)
# - /loot give lines name each block ("Gave 1 [Spruce Tavern Table]", ...)
# - "[DevScript] actionbar: To the Barrow" (the named signpost) and "WANTED, dead or alive: Grimtusk ..." (sketch 1)
# - after the bedroll sleep: "Long rest: health, mana and charges restored", the sleeper's y at 150.3125 while
#   asleep, and "Found no elements matching SpawnX" (no spawn point)
# Screenshots: shared-blocks-furniture, shared-blocks-signpost-a/-b (both faces of the dungeon one),
# shared-blocks-signpost-named, shared-blocks-poster, shared-blocks-bedrolls.
