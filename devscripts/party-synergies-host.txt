#  4 Bodyguard: 12 husks hit the host; ~30% switch to the guest Fighter ("retarget"), target readout agrees
#  5 Opportunist (Wizard host): +15% on the husks that are on the guest Tank only
#  6 Balanced party: 2 roles -> x1.0
#  7 Scout: the guest sees a seal's hint from 9.5 blocks only once the host is an Artificer (Utility)
