# Timed areas (SkillHelpers.TimedArea), tracked projectiles and cooldowns (SkillHelpers.Cooldowns).
# Run: timeout 420 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/skill-helpers-check.txt
# Expect in latest.log ([CHAT] lines, in order):
#  1. Rain of Arrows: "Test passed, count: 118" (2 arrows a tick for 59 ticks, none hit anything).
#  2. Hellgate: husk hn Health below 200 and on fire; husk hf (9 blocks out) 200.0f. After the ring ends
#     (t=220, t=260) hn only loses what it takes from still burning (about 1).
#  3. Wither Cloud: husk cn has Wither (Id 20) in ActiveEffects; husk cf (7 blocks out) has none.
#  4. Meteor Swarm: husk mz Health below 200.
#  5. Volatile Flask: husk vz Health below 200.
#  6. Quivering Palm: husk qz Health drops by more than 20 from one punch.
#  7. Second Wind (max health is above 30, so 16 damage isn't under a quarter yet): 9.0f, 9.0f, then 3.0f and
#     9.0f (healed 6), then 3.0f again and still 3.0f 40 ticks later (1200-tick cooldown).
#  8. Divine Shield: knockback resistance 1.0 while up, 0.0 once it ends (300 ticks).
# Screenshots: sh-rain, sh-hellgate, sh-cloud, sh-meteor

/dndclass set @s ranger
wait 10
closescreen
/clear @s dndclasses:class_guidebook
/dndclass resetprogress @s
/dndclass xp @s set 2700
/dndclass unlock @s ranger.rain_of_arrows
/dndclass equip @s ranger.rain_of_arrows
/dndclass mana @s 9
/dndclass charges @s 9
/tp @s 102.5 150 115.5 -90 9
wait 10

press key.dnd-classes.power-up
wait 30

wait 50
/execute if entity @e[type=minecraft:arrow,tag=dndclasses.ranger_rain]
/kill @e[type=minecraft:arrow]
perspective first
