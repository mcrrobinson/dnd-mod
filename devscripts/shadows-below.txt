# Chain "Shadows Below" (docs/quests/quests.md, design 9 T10): the Dwarf King -> the duergar factor -> the Elf Speaker.
# Runs the chain with /quest admin and real steps where they're cheap: /underdark go for the visit, real Holds,
# Outposts and the Spire from /dndhome, the factor's check with /quest admin ... pass persuasion, the Speaker's Insight
# topic with a forced 20, a summoned Matron killed with /damage.
# Run: timeout 900 ./gradlew runClient --offline -PdevUser=ShadowDev -PdevScript=devscripts/shadows-below.txt
# Expect in latest.log:
# - "[Quests] ... dndclasses:shadows_below/1: revealed dndclasses:underdark_rift" on accept, then "stage 1/3 complete"
#   right after /underdark go and "revealed dndclasses:duergar_hold at ..." (a normal map: you're in the Underdark)
# - "stage 2/3 complete" at the Hold's gate, "stage 3/3 complete" after the persuasion pass
# - "Test failed" for an emerald after talking to the factor (quest 1 pays at the King), "Test failed" for a sigil
#   after killing drow at an outpost before quest 2, then "Test passed" for exactly 3 sigils (and failed for 4)
#   after 14 kills on quest 2, "Test passed" for 24 emeralds from the factor, then 24+32 after the King
# - "[Quests] ... dndclasses:drow_spire at ... in dndclasses:underdark for ShadowDev (rift map)", "Test passed" for a
#   map with dndclassesRiftTarget
# - "stage 3/3 complete" for shadows_below/3 on the Matron kill, "Test passed" for the matron_slain advancement and 64
#   emeralds at the Speaker; /rep shows Sylvan Court +300 (+200, +100 for an elf) over the value before
# Screenshots: sb-1-offer, sb-2-hold, sb-3-factor-offer, sb-4-sigils, sb-5-king-elves, sb-6-speaker-offer,
