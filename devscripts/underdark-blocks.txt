# Checks the Underdark's natural blocks (docs/items/underdark-blocks.md): a showcase, drops, real breaking with the
# right tools, the Zurkhwood Planks shapes and the stem -> planks recipe, Bluecap placement, and the Nightlight Cap's
# light.
# Run: timeout 300 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/underdark-blocks.txt
# Expect in latest.log:
# - each /loot line drops its own block ("Dropped 1 [...]"), the slab two when double and the door once
# - after breaking stem, cap, loam, faerzress stone and a bluecap by hand/tool and picking them up, `slots` lists
#   each one in the inventory
# - the crafting table: one Zurkhwood Stem in the grid gives 4 Zurkhwood Planks in slot 0
# - "Test passed" for the bluecap placed on Fungal Loam in full daylight (still there 200 ticks later), and
#   "Test failed" for one used on sand (it can't stand there)
# - screenshot underdark-blocks-showcase, and underdark-nightlight-light (F3: block light 12 one block from a cap
#   in the Underdark, where there's no sky light)
