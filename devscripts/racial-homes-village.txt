# Racial homes at the dev world's hobbit village (inn box 175 67 1327 to 189 79 1337, innkeeper at 181.5 67 1330.5).
# Run on a fresh copy of run/: timeout 400 ./gradlew runClient -PdevRunDir=run-rh -PdevScript=devscripts/racial-homes-village.txt
# Expect in latest.log:
# - Human: no "[RacialHomes] Welcomed", empty inventory, innkeeper offers at base price.
# - Halfling: one "Welcomed" line, a cake, 4 cookies and 2 ale (basket), /dndhome "hearth true", Regeneration in
#   ActiveEffects, offers 25% cheaper ("trade 1: 1 ... (base 1, ...)" stays 1, "trade 6: 1 emerald (base 2, ...)", wheat 13 of 20: the kin -5 plus Friendly -2).
# - Leaving and coming back at once: no second welcome or basket.
# - A frozen spider inside 16 blocks: no Regeneration; after killing it, Regeneration again.
# - A hobbit 3500 ticks into its 6000-tick gift cooldown gives a Halfling a snack, refuses a Human.
# - /rep: Halfling starts Friendly (150) with the Hobbits of the Shire.

/fill 178 149 1322 184 149 1328 minecraft:stone
/fill 178 150 1322 184 153 1328 minecraft:air

/data get entity @s Inventory
/dndhome
/rep
/summon dndclasses:innkeeper 179.5 150 1326.5 {NoAI:1b,Tags:["rhinn"],Rotation:[180f,0f]}
/tp @s 179.5 150 1324.5 0 15

wait 40
use
wait 10

closescreen
