# Checks identification, curses and Remove Curse (docs/systems/magic-items.md). Run: timeout 600 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/curses.txt
# Expect in latest.log ([CHAT] lines), in order:
#  1. Fighter: an unidentified cursed (Frailty) iron helmet put on: "A curse takes hold", /dndmagic bonds lists it "cursed",
#     max health 20 -> 16, and the helmet can't be taken off (slots still lists it in slot 5)
#  2. Cleric self Remove Curse, forced 2: "[D20] ... Remove Curse 2 +5 = 7 vs DC 10 -> FAILURE", the bond stays
#  3. 60 s later, forced 15: "-> SUCCESS", "you're no longer bound", 0 bonds, max health 20, the helmet off the head
#  4. Wizard picks up an unidentified cursed +2 sword: "Your arcane training reveals: [+2 Diamond Sword] (Rare, cursed: Bloodthirst)"
#  5. Fighter reads a Scroll of Identify with an unidentified +1 sword in the offhand: "The scroll reveals"
#  6. The table's Identify button spends a scroll: "The scroll reveals"; with none: "uses a Scroll of Identify. You have none."
#  7. Short rest: "While you rest, you study your loot"
#  8. Innkeeper, sneak + right-click with 4 emeralds and an Uncommon in the offhand: "The innkeeper squints at it", emeralds gone
#  9. Gluttony: Hunger I for 100 ticks adds about 0.75 exhaustion instead of 0.5; Scroll of Remove Curse frees it
# 10. Sun-sick: Weakness (Id 18) at noon outdoors
# 11. Ill Omen: /dndclass sheet shows the disadvantage; a forced 2 on a WIS save is a FUMBLE with "dis"
# 12. Bloodthirst: holding a cursed sword 3 s binds it; 60 s later 1 damage (Health 20 -> 19)
# 13. Beacon: a vindicator 20 blocks away only comes for you once you're cursed
# 14. Loot: 34 rolls of dndclasses:magic/uncommon, some with a curse (grep the Inventory dump for curse:)
