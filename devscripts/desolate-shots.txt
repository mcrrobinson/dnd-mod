# Screenshots of every desolate settlement at every desolation level, in fresh land east of x 100000.
# Run: timeout 1800 ./gradlew runClient -PdevScript=devscripts/desolate-shots.txt (move the start points for a
# world where these have generated already).
# Checks: screenshots desolate-<level>-<structure> (aerial) and -low; "Test failed" for each entity check (no
# entities round the settlement) and "[<mob>] here" for any there are; "[Desolation] ... (<LEVEL>)" lines with
# the entities dropped, containers emptied, blocks withered and breaches per chunk in <run dir>/logs/debug.log.
