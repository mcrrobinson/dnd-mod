# Cogsdeep
The gnomes' burrow under the forests and meadows. On the surface there's only a round observatory of gnomish bricks and brass under a copper dome that weathers green, with a telescope poking out of a slit in the dome. Inside it, a spiral stair winds down a sealed shaft to the burrow at y 11-30: the Commons round the foot of the stair, two workshop halls full of contraptions, a clocktower room, a gem-polishing room and a lush grotto. It's built from the gnomes' own [building blocks](cogsdeep-blocks.md): riveted brass, gnomish bricks, turning cogwork and gears, pipework, glowcrystals and a clock face that keeps the time. [Gnome Tinkers and Clockwork Sentinels](../mobs/gnomes.md) live here. Cogsdeep is the Gnome home (see [Racial homes](../races/racial-homes.md)).

## How it works
Every burrow is the same plan, turned so the observatory door faces one of the four directions. Local layout below ground, with the door to the south:

```
                  clocktower (13 x 17, 14 high)
west workshop  <-  Commons (15 x 15)  ->  east workshop  ->  lush grotto
                  gem room (13 x 15)
```

| Piece | What it is |
|-|-|
| Observatory | On the ground: a round tower of Gnomish Bricks 13 across, 5 high with glass windows, framed by a Riveted Brass footing and a Cut Riveted Brass course under the dome, on a foundation of Gnomish Bricks down to the ground. Where the ground before the door rises, a way is cut through it. A hemispherical dome of cut copper (some of it already exposed, weathered or oxidised; unwaxed, so it keeps going green) with a lightning rod on top, and a slit 3 blocks wide open to the north from just above the walls up over the top. Against the north wall, a spruce gallery 5 blocks up (on two log posts, railed with fences, a ladder up the wall) holds the telescope: an end-rod eyepiece at head height and a tube of 3 lightning rods ending in a Telescope Lens on a fence tripod, level with the bottom of the slit and running out through it, so its last rod and the lens poke out of the dome; a spruce stool beside it. On the floor: a spruce floor with a Cut Riveted Brass ring, a cartography desk with a spyglass lying on it, a lectern, bookshelves, a chest and a barrel. A spruce door to the south under a blue banner with the gold [Gnome Cog](../items/banner-patterns.md), lanterns on Gnomish Brick Wall posts, and a dirt path. Trees and plants round it are cleared. |
| Spiral stair | A sealed shaft 7 x 7 from the observatory floor to the Commons: a 3 x 3 Gnomish Brick pillar with glowstone set in it every 5 steps, and a one-wide Gnomish Brick stair winding round it, 16 steps a turn (15 blocks of headroom). Every rise is half a block, so you walk up and down it without jumping: a corner step faces the way you come in going up, so its raised half is on the far side of the turn, and the landing at the top is a spruce stair onto the observatory floor. At the top a fence rails the opening except for a one-block gap onto the landing by the first step, and a copper block with a lightning rod sits on the pillar. Doorways to the four rooms. |
| Workshops | East and west of the Commons, each 17 x 11, 6 high, with a smooth stone aisle, a band of Cut Riveted Brass round the walls under the ceiling and two runs of Pipework along it. | Clocktower | North of the Commons: 13 x 17, 14 high. The far wall holds a clock face 11 across: calcite with a Cut Riveted Brass rim, glowstone marks at the quarters, blackstone hands at ten to twelve and a Clockface at the hub that shows the real time. A Riveted Brass pendulum on a long chain, a bell from the ceiling, three rows of turning Gearwall along both side walls, a checker floor of Gnomish Bricks and deepslate tiles. | Gem room | South of the Commons: 13 x 15, 5 high, a calcite and smooth basalt checker floor. Every room is a sealed shell: walls, floor and ceiling are always built whole, so no water, lava or gravel from the caves and aquifers round it gets in, and the burrow is never open to a cave. Real lush caves are rare under forests, so the gnomes grow their own.

### People
At generation: 4 Gnome Tinkers (the Commons, both workshops, the gem room) and 5 Clockwork Sentinels (the Commons, both workshops, the clocktower, the grotto). Both kinds also spawn naturally on the burrow floor in the rooms (not on the stair or in the observatory), up to 10 gnomes and Sentinels within 48 blocks. No monsters spawn anywhere in a burrow's box. See [Gnomes](../mobs/gnomes.md).

### The Tinkers' law
Friendly to everyone. Every gnome and Sentinel within 16 blocks (24 if you're Unfriendly or worse with the Tinkers' Guild) who can see you takes offence, and the Sentinels turn on you (until you get away, then 20-39 s more), if anywhere in the burrow you:

- open or break a chest or barrel
- break one of the contraptions: a piston, sticky piston, observer, redstone lamp, dispenser, dropper, hopper, repeater, comparator, note block or lightning rod, or the burrow's clockwork: a Cogwork Panel, Gearwall, Clockface, Glowcrystal, Pipework, Blueprint Table or Telescope Lens

Each seen offence costs 40 standing with the Tinkers' Guild. Striking a gnome or a Sentinel brings every Sentinel within 16 blocks down on you.

A Gnome's first offence each in-game day is let off with "The Sentinel's gears grind, but it lets it pass." (kin trust).

## For your race
Cogsdeep is the Gnome home. A Gnome gets:

- a "Welcome home, Gnome" title on arriving (anywhere over the burrow's area counts, the observatory included)
- Regeneration I in the Commons while no hostile mob is within 16 blocks
- 25% off every Tinker's trades
- Kin stock: every Tinker sells Gnomes the [Gnomish Repeater](../items/racial-weapons.md#gnomish-repeater) (14 emeralds) and [Tinker's Goggles](../items/tinkers-goggles.md) (10) before kin prices; nobody else sees those trades
- kin trust (one warning a day)
- an Honored (450) start with the Tinkers' Guild; everyone else starts Friendly (150)

## Where to find it
Forests, flower forests and meadows (`#dndclasses:has_structure/cogsdeep`). Burrows use a spacing of 28 chunks and a separation of 10, and stay 5 chunks from vanilla villages (the structure set's exclusion zone). The site check:

- the ground in the middle must be between y 58 and 150, and 16 points on rings 3 and 7 blocks out must be dry and within 3 blocks of it
- the burrow's floor is rolled between y 11 and 16 (the clocktower's ceiling is then at most y 30)
- the rock where the rooms go (8 noise columns, before cave carvers) must be at least 70% solid, so the burrow is never hung in a huge cave
- no hobbit village, elven enclave, goblin camp, crypt, goblin warren, dwarven ruin, dwarven fortress (they share the meadows) or beholder lair (its stair climbs past the burrow's depth) may start within 5 chunks; only ones that really generate there count

Surveys of 3 x 3 points 2000 blocks apart in fresh parts of the dev world found 5 to 8 burrows each, in forests, flower forests and meadows; the last one, round 90000 90000, passed 5 of 5.

## Commands
- `/locate structure dndclasses:cogsdeep`
- `/dndhome cogsdeep [door|top|observatory|ladder|telescope|slit|stair|foot|commons|workshop|westworkshop|clocktower|strongbox|gems|grotto]` (operators): teleports you to that spot of the nearest burrow, looking at it. `top` is 22 blocks above the observatory; `ladder` is at the foot of the gallery's ladder, `telescope` on the gallery beside the eyepiece and `slit` in the air outside the dome, looking at the telescope's end; `stair` is halfway down the stair; `foot` is in the Commons by the stair's last steps.
- `/dndhome cogsdeepsurvey [grid] [spacing]` (operators): finds the nearest burrow from each point of a grid round you (default 3 x 3, 2000 blocks apart), generates it, and checks the biome at the door, all 7 piece kinds, the burrow floor at y 11-16 with the roof at most y 30, the ground 8 blocks out round the observatory within 3 blocks of its floor (at most 4 of 16 points off), no water in the rooms (the grotto's pool aside), and no other structure starting within 5 chunks (mineshafts, ruined portals and the like aside). Each burrow logs `[CogsdeepSurvey] cogsdeep at X Y Z: biome ..., 8 pieces (7 kinds), burrow floor y F (roof R), ground off by >3 at N/16, water in rooms 0, T tinkers, S sentinels, neighbours within 5 chunks []: PASS` (or FAIL), then a summary.
- `/dndhome pieces` lists every piece of the burrow you're in.

## Known limitations
- Goblins don't raid Cogsdeep: a war party couldn't reach a burrow down a one-wide spiral stair, and the gnomes' folk would never see it at the hatch. The design doesn't call for it.
- Trees that grow in the neighbouring chunks after the observatory is built can hang a few leaves over its dome.
- The burrow's box counts as Cogsdeep all the way up to the ground, so the welcome title shows in the forest above the rooms too.
- No quests or bounties for the Tinkers' Guild yet (the Tinkers have the quest-giver role `gnome_tinker` ready).

## For developers
